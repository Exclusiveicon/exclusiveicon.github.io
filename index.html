D-Self Ads Reader

D-Self Ads Reader is an internal tool used only by Davidson Self Storage. It reads advertising performance reports (campaigns, daily spend, clicks, impressions, conversions, keywords and search terms) from Davidson Self Storage's own Google Ads account so the owner can review advertising results.

It is not offered to the public, and it does not create, change, pause or delete any campaign, budget or setting.
