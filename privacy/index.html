Privacy Policy — D-Self Ads Reader

Last updated: October 21, 2026

D-Self Ads Reader ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Davidson Self Storage. It is used only with Davidson Self Storage's own Google account and Google Ads account.

What the app accesses. With the account owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app reads Google Ads reporting data for Davidson Self Storage's own Ads account: campaign names and status, and daily spend, clicks, impressions, conversion value, keywords, search terms, landing-page addresses, targeted locations, basic account details, and the list of users on the Ads account with their access levels (to confirm the app's own access is read-only). It also reads and keeps the signed-in account's email address, to confirm that the correct account was used.

How it is used. The data is used only to report advertising results to Davidson Self Storage's owner, including summaries prepared by Davidson Self Storage's own AI assistant. The app never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Ads.

Storage and security. The reporting data is stored on Davidson Self Storage's own computer. The Google sign-in credential is kept in a separate credential vault on that computer, used only for requests to Google, and is never shown to the AI assistant or anyone else.

Sharing. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used by Davidson Self Storage to train AI models. Report data may be processed by the AI service provider that runs Davidson Self Storage's assistant (currently Anthropic), solely to prepare these internal summaries.

Google API Services. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. The account owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps & services → D-Self Ads Reader → Remove. Removing access stops future reads; reports already stored stay until Davidson Self Storage deletes them, which it will do on request to the contact address below.
